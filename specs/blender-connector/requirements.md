| `CONN-BLENDER-CONNECTOR-R001` | **Declare the `mcp-for-blender` stdio upstream.** Subsystem: upstream declarations. `blender_connector/upstreams.py` holds the tuple `UPSTREAMS` of one `UpstreamDecl` value (`SCION-CONNECTOR-CONTROL-R914.1`).
